## 🎞️ Creating your first animation

Open a new project to start working. Drag your media files into the project bin. From there, move your files onto the timeline.

Follow these basic steps to animate an object:

1. Select an object on the stage.
2. Move the playhead to the desired start point on the timeline.
3. Click the keyframe button to set your starting position.
4. Move the playhead forward in time to the next point.
5. Move the object on the stage to its new position.
6. The software generates a path between these two points.
7. Press the spacebar to preview the motion.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting common issues

If you encounter technical performance problems, check these solutions:

* Software fails to open: Ensure you have administrative rights on your user account. Restart your computer and try again.
* Preview stutters: Lower the quality of the preview window. Remove heavy visual effects until you finish your final export.
* Export takes too long: Close other background applications while the software renders your project.
* Missing files: Ensure the original audio or image files remain in their original folders. Renaming or moving these files causes errors in your project timeline.

If the software continues to crash, verify that your graphics drivers are updated to the latest version. Visit your computer manufacturer website to obtain the correct driver updates for your hardware. Keep your Windows environment updated for the best compatibility.
